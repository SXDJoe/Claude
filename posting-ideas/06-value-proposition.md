## Evaluation

This is the most important item on the page and it is not a posting idea. It is the condition the posting ideas depend on.

Every other topic here is an observation about how organisations fail. Observations without a stated offer produce a specific outcome: the reader agrees, the reader does not act, and you are read as a commentator rather than hired as an advisor. Commentary and advisory are different businesses and the content cannot serve both.

The quantification question is the harder one and you flagged it correctly. Governance value is counterfactual: the cost avoided, the decision not delayed, the programme not restarted. Counterfactuals do not survive a CFO conversation. The way out is not to quantify the governance. It is to quantify the failure you prevent, using the client's own numbers from a programme that already went wrong, and to let the reader do the subtraction. Cost of a six month delay on a programme of a stated size is a number a buyer accepts, because they supplied it.

A warning on the goal as written. "How do I provide value" is the wrong question to lead with. It is inward facing and it has no boundary, so it produces a list of everything you can do. The question that produces an offer is narrower: which decision is a buyer already stuck on, and what do they do differently after one engagement with you.

## Consequence if left unresolved

The sequence in `00-index.md` can still be written and it will perform well as content. It will not convert. Posts one to three build the problem, and there is nothing at the end of the story for the reader to walk into.

## Recommendation

Resolve this before the first post goes out, not after. One sentence: for whom, which stuck decision, what changes after. Then one number from real work that shows the cost of not having it.
