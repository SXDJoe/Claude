# Reinvention, and the book question

Source: `Posting Ideas.pdf`, page 2. "Reinventing, book [?]". The question mark is on the page.
Status: format decision, not a topic.

## What the note says

A theme, reinvention, and an open question about whether it becomes a book.

## Evaluation

Two separate things are stuck together here and both are weakened by it.

Reinvention as a theme is unbounded. It has no owner, no failure mode and no buyer attached to it in this note. In its current state it is a mood, not a topic. It becomes a topic only when it is pointed at something: reinvention of an operating model, reinvention blocked by governance, reinvention as the thing the paradox prevents. Unpointed, it will produce the most generic posts in the set.

The book question is a capital allocation decision that does not belong in a posting list. A book is a year of attention and it prices your positioning for a decade. Deciding it before a single post has been tested is a bet on an untested thesis, and the cost of being wrong is not the writing time, it is the year you did not spend on the offer.

The sequence is the other way around. Post the thesis, in parts, for a defined period. If a specific idea produces inbound conversations and not just agreement, the book has a proven spine and an audience. If nothing produces inbound, the book would not have either, and you saved the year.

## Recommendation

Drop the book question from this folder and re-open it after the first sequence has run. Keep reinvention only as a frame attached to a named subject.
