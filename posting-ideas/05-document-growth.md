# Document growth

Source: `Posting Ideas.pdf`, page 2. Two words, no context.
Status: ambiguous, needs your reading before anything is built on it.

## What the note says

"Document growth". The page supports two readings and does not settle between them.

Reading A: document your own growth publicly, building SXD in the open.
Reading B: documentation as a discipline, how a transformation programme records what it decides and how that record grows or decays.

## Evaluation

Under reading A this is weak for your position. Building in public works for founders whose audience buys the story. Your buyers are executives who hire on the basis of judgement already demonstrated elsewhere, and growth documentation from a solo advisory firm reads as either small numbers or self-promotion. There is one version that works: publishing what a specific decision in your own business cost you and what you would do differently. That is a judgement demonstration wearing a growth post.

Under reading B it is a strong support piece and a poor headline. Nobody responds to a post about documentation. It earns its place as evidence inside a decision governance post: the decision record is where you show that nobody owned the decision.

Either way it is not a lead topic. Reading A needs a hard rule about which numbers are public. Reading B folds into file 02.

## Open question for you

Which reading did you mean. If A, the rule on numbers has to exist first.
